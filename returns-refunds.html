Returns, Refunds & Cancellation

Return and refund eligibility depends on product condition, seller policy, delivery status and applicable Nepal consumer law.

Cancellation

Cancellation may be requested before fulfilment where the order status allows it. A completed payment should not be treated as refunded until the refund is verified and recorded.

Returns

Products should be returned according to the applicable product/seller return terms. Customers should keep order and transaction references.

Refunds

Approved refunds should be processed through the applicable payment method or another authorized method, with a refund reference recorded.

Disputes

Customers may contact FaishonNepal support regarding unresolved order, return or refund issues.
